Booking FAQ

How long is a session?

Sessions are booked in four-hour blocks, with a four-hour minimum.

Is Lewis involved in my session?

Yes. Lewis's engineering and production time is included in every session.

How do I reserve a session?

Send an inquiry first. Once Lewis confirms your session, a 50% deposit reserves it. The remaining 50% is due on the day of the session.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

The deposit is non-refundable if you cancel. If you reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice, your deposit can be applied to the new session.

What if we need more time?

Additional time is agreed with Lewis before it's added, and any extra charge is made clear before you commit.

How can I pay?

Cash, Zelle, Venmo or check. Payment details come from Lewis once your session is confirmed.

Who owns the recordings and how are credits handled?

Rights and credits are agreed directly with Lewis for each project, separately from the session fee. Nothing about ownership or credit is assumed by booking.

Will my session appear on your social media?

Only with your separate, explicit permission for specific content. Booking a session doesn't give the studio permission to post you or your music.
